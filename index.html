The Wall
Individual Art Class Project
February – June, 2024
Wood, Strings, Arduino Board,
Motors, Acrylic Paint, Tools
Rhino, Enscape
36" × 48" × 3"

The development of technology encouraged more human communications without physical interactions, making face to face conversations harder and more precious. Inspired by the movement logic of plant stomata, the Wall is created to improve in person communications and social engagements. It opens and closes according to human emotions.

The Globe
Individual Work
September – December, 2023
PVC board, Paper, Pen
Rhino, Grasshopper, Photoshop
Vary in size

All activities at the Brooks School surround two roads: the main street and another street. Life as a student at Brooks starts at the crossing of two roads. Life around the main street can be inconvenient and time consuming. Hence, a retractable hemisphere tent is designed to be placed around campus.
